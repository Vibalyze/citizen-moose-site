Citizen Moose: Canada Test

Pass your Canadian citizenship test

A free study app for the Canadian citizenship test, in English and French. No ads, no subscription, no account.

What's inside

  • 583 practice questions on every topic of the study guide Discover Canada, each with a short explanation, the guide's own words and a source.
  • Today's 20, a timed practice test (20 questions, 45 minutes, 15 to pass) and an endless shuffle by topic.
  • My mistakes: questions you get wrong stay there until you answer them right.
  • The study guide's text, offline, with IRCC's free audio.
  • My Canada (who's who in your province), a map of the provinces and territories, a history timeline, and the oath and ceremony.

Where the questions come from

IRCC does not publish its test questions. Ours are practice questions written from the facts in Discover Canada or adapted from free study projects, each checked against the guide and today's facts. When the guide is out of date (for example 308 ridings, now 343), the app says what the guide says and what is true today.
