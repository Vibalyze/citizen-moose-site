Citizen Moose: Canada Test

Privacy

Citizen Moose for iPhone · Updated 23 November 2026 (version 1.0.0)

We collect no personal data. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone. The app sends a few anonymous statistics so we know what helps people study; you can turn them off in Settings. If you write to us from the app, we receive your message.

Who we are

The app is published by Vibalyze OÜ, Järvevana tee 9, 11314 Tallinn, Estonia (registry code 17240903). Contact: vibalyze@zohomail.eu.

What the app sends

Anonymous statistics. The app sends short events to our own server, for example "today's 20 finished, 16/20", "practice test finished, 17/20, passed" or "province chosen: Ontario". Each event holds only numbers and fixed labels chosen by the app (province, language, topic, type of round), plus the app version, the phone's system and version, language and time zone, and the country of the connection.

  • Events are tied only to a random install id created on your phone (and a random id per session). It is not your name, your Apple account, a device id or the advertising id. It is replaced after 13 months.
  • Question text, your answers, your test date and anything you type (including the names you write in My Canada) are never part of an event.
  • The server derives the country from the connection; it never stores your IP address.
  • Raw events are deleted after 120 days. Daily totals, with no id at all, are kept for 25 months.
  • Offline, events wait on the phone (1,000 at most) and are sent later. The app works fully offline.
  • To turn them off: Settings › Anonymous statistics. Turning off deletes waiting events and nothing more is sent.

Anonymous error reports. If the app hits a software error, it sends an "error" event with the error type, a short fingerprint and the names of our code files and lines involved, so we can fix it. The error message is never sent, nor anything else from your phone. These go through the same anonymous statistics, with the same id, retention and switch.

Install origin (iPhone). On first launch the app asks Apple's AdServices for a token and sends it once to our server, to learn whether the install came from an Apple Search Ads campaign (campaign, keyword, country and date). This is Apple's privacy-preserving attribution: no advertising id, nothing about you, no tracking, so no tracking permission is asked. If statistics are off, nothing is sent.

"Write to us". If you send a message from Settings, we receive the message, its type (problem, idea or other), the app and system details above, the install id (to link a problem to what happened) and your email only if you choose to give it for a reply. Messages are kept 24 months. This works even with statistics off, since you send it on purpose.

Our server runs on Cloudflare, with the database in the European Union. Cloudflare processes this data for us as a provider. We do not sell any data, share it with advertisers or brokers, or track you across other apps or sites.

What we never collect

No name, no contacts, no location, no photos, no microphone, no camera, no advertising id or device id. The app contains no advertising tools, no third-party crash reporter and no social network login.

What stays on your phone

Your language and province, your answers, mistakes, practice tests and streak, your test date if you enter it, the names you type in My Canada, your map progress and your ceremony checklist are saved in the app's private storage. None of it is sent to us. It follows your iPhone's normal backup (iCloud or computer), managed by Apple. Deleting the app deletes it.

Reminders

The daily reminder is off until you allow it. If you turn it on, iOS asks for permission, then the notification is scheduled on your phone only: no server sends it.

Links

Links and the guide's audio open in your browser and are governed by those sites' policies.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is meant for adults preparing for the citizenship test. It collects no personal data about anyone, children included.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete data. Deleting the app deletes your progress, the install id and waiting events. To have the statistics and messages of your install deleted from our server, write to us and we'll help you find and delete them. You can also contact the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee) or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

Changes

If the app changes how it handles data, this page is updated before that version is released.
