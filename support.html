Citizen Moose: Canada Test

Support

Found a wrong answer, an out-of-date fact or a bug? Tell us: in the app, Settings › Write to us, or by email at vibalyze@zohomail.eu. Each question also has a report button.

Questions

Are these the real test questions? No. They are practice questions on the facts in the study guide.

The guide says something different. The guide was written in 2009-2012. Where a fact changed (for example 343 ridings, King Charles III), the app shows today's fact and what the guide says.

Do I need an account or internet? No account. Everything works offline except the audio.
